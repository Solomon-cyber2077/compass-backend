Guidelines & privacy

Community guidelines

The Compass works because people who have walked a path leave honest notes for the people behind them. Keep it that way:

  1. Be specific when you ask. Your field, your level, what you've tried, and what decision the answer will help you make.
  2. Answer from experience. Say what you have actually seen or done. If you are guessing, say so.
  3. TMs: never overstate your experience. Your role and years are shown on every note. Misrepresenting them gets the badge removed.
  4. Keep confidential things confidential. No employer secrets, private salary data about named people, or anyone's personal information. Blur personal details in photos.
  5. No spam, self-promotion, harassment, or discrimination.
  6. Career advice, not professional advice. Notes are personal experience, not legal, financial, or medical advice.

See something that breaks these? Use Report on the question or note. Admins review every report and remove what breaks the rules.

What we store

  • Your account: username, display name, and a securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself).
  • Your email, if you add one: used only for password resets and the notifications you turn on. It is never shown on the site.
  • TM applications: your role, years of experience, and description are shown publicly once you're approved. Your proof link is seen only by admins.
  • What you post: questions, notes, photos, links, and upvotes. Questions and notes are public; who upvoted what is not.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

You can delete any of your questions and notes at any time, and delete your whole account from your account page. Deleting your account removes your email, settings, and upvotes; questions and notes you didn't delete first stay up but show as "[deleted]".

What is The Compass?

Everyone entering a new career faces the same trial. Most of the people who got through it once stood exactly where you are.

The Compass is where they leave notes for their successors: straight answers to your questions, without the cold-message silence of LinkedIn.

Temporary Mentors

A TM is someone with real, first-hand experience in the field. Every TM applies with proof of their experience and is approved by a reviewer before they get the badge. Their role and years of experience show on every note.

Been through it? Apply to be a TM, then open any question marked Needs a TM.

House rules

  1. Be specific. Say your field, level, and what you've tried.
  2. TMs: only claim experience you actually have.
  3. No confidential employer information.
  4. Be the mentor you wish you'd had.
